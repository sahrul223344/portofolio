About Me

I'm Sahrul, an Informatics student and passionate Full-Stack Web Developer based in Indonesia. My journey in technology is driven by a deep curiosity for solving real-world challenges through clean code, scalable architecture, and intuitive user interface designs.

Over the course of my academic and practical experience, I have developed expertise across modern web stacks — spanning from front-end user experience engineering with React and Tailwind CSS to robust back-end systems using FastAPI and PHP. I specialize in integrating generative AI models (such as LLMs and Speech Synthesis pipelines) into seamless web applications.
